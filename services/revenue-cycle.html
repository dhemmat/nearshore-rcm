BOX 3

Work performed

What actually happens, daily and weekly — inside your systems, on US hours

  • Claims scrubbed and submitted daily — not batched into next week
  • Rejections worked same-day from the clearinghouse queue
  • Denials triaged by reason code with appeals filed before timely-filing deadlines
  • Payer calls made live during Eastern business hours
  • Payments posted with contractual variances flagged, not buried
  • Eligibility verified before the visit, not after the denial
  • Prior auths submitted and tracked to decision
  • A/R worked oldest-first with notes your team can audit
  • Patient balance workflows handled with native-Spanish capability where needed
  • End-of-day production notes so nothing is a black box
