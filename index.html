BOX 2

Revenue cycle labor is your largest controllable administrative cost

Itemization of charges — what the function costs to staff in the US

Billing, A/R, denials, credentialing, and payer follow-up repeat every month, forever. The work is permanent — what it costs to staff is not. US healthcare organizations pay high fully-loaded rates per seat, absorb constant turnover, and still watch denial queues go unworked.

Reason · CO-29 timely filing expired

The denial pile

Denied claims that never get worked are pure loss. Most organizations recover less than half of what is actually appealable — not because appeals fail, but because nobody has the hours to file them before deadlines pass.

Loss code · Internal — Turnover

The turnover tax

Every biller departure costs three to six months of degraded A/R while you recruit, hire, and train a replacement. At team scale, someone is always leaving.

Loss code · Internal — Wrong-wage work

The distraction

When clinicians and practice leadership absorb billing tasks, you pay clinical and executive wages for clerical work — and every job involved gets done worse.

BOX 9

Privacy & security program

Blue Coding · SOC 2 attested

Revenue cycle work makes a service provider a HIPAA Business Associate — we treat that as an operating model, stated factually

9.1

BAA before any PHI access

A Business Associate Agreement executed with every client — and subcontractor BAAs where required — as a precondition, not an option.

9.2

Least-privilege access, your credentials

Accounts you provision, scoped to the role, with MFA enforced, prompt deprovisioning, and a revocation switch that is always yours. Controlled access to client systems is preferred over any local PHI handling.

9.3

Company-managed equipment

Managed, encrypted, patched devices. Restrictions on local downloads, printing, personal email, and removable media. No personal devices touch client systems.

9.4

Training and screening

Formal HIPAA privacy and security training with documented completion for every team member before PHI access, plus background checks appropriate to each delivery country.

9.5

Risk analysis and audit trails

Written policies, security risk analysis with ongoing risk management, activity logging, and documented incident-response and breach-escalation procedures.

9.6

Two-country continuity

Bogotá and Santo Domingo operations provide US-hours coverage with built-in geographic redundancy — an outage in one location does not stop your revenue cycle.

We describe our safeguards factually and claim nothing we cannot substantiate. NearshoreRCM operates under Blue Coding, which holds SOC 2 attestation; our controls are built to that same standard, and extending the attestation’s scope to cover NearshoreRCM’s healthcare operations is on the compliance roadmap.
